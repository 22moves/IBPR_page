What Happens Between the Numbers?

Financial results and standard reports rarely show the full picture of everyday operations. These are questions many businesses find hard to answer:

  • Where are potential customers and requests being lost?
  • Which processes take more time than expected?
  • What information is collected but never used?
  • Which decisions depend on incomplete or outdated information?
  • Where are people or equipment underused?
  • Which problems only become visible after they have caused losses?
  • What important events are not being measured at all?

Five areas of research

Customer & Commercial Processes

How customers arrive, buy, and return.

Lost requests, sales performance, retention, customer behavior.

Operations & Workflows

How work actually moves through the business.

Delays, duplicated work, bottlenecks, manual operations.

People & Organizational Processes

How effort and information are shared between people.

Workload distribution, dependencies, internal information exchange.

Assets & Infrastructure

How equipment and physical conditions affect results.

Utilization, downtime, energy use, sensor-based monitoring.

Data & Decision Intelligence

Whether the right information reaches the right decision.

Data availability and quality, reporting, forecasting, metrics.

How we research

We understand how a business works before proposing any technology.

  1. Discover

    Understand the business, its goals, existing processes, and operational challenges.

  2. Map

    Identify information sources, manual actions, dependencies, missing data, and bottlenecks.

  3. Evaluate

    Decide which problems could benefit from better measurement, data collection, integration, or automation.

  4. Develop

    Explore and test suitable solutions, from simple workflow changes to custom data systems, intelligent agents, and sensor monitoring.

From research to technology

Technology is a possible method, not the starting point. These are approaches we may explore once a problem is understood.

Data Collection

Record information that businesses do not currently measure.

System Integration

Connect existing software, databases, spreadsheets, and business applications.

Workflow Automation

Reduce repetitive manual work and automate predictable processes.

AI & Intelligent Agents

Extract, classify, and organize information from messages, documents, and other unstructured sources.

Smart Monitoring

Use sensors and connected devices to understand equipment, environments, and resource use.

Analytics & Reporting

Turn raw information into indicators, reports, predictions, and alerts.

Research Before Solutions.

IBPR is an independent research initiative exploring how businesses collect information, manage operations, and make decisions.

We are interested in the gap between how organizations operate and how well they can observe, measure, and improve their processes.

Our research begins with real operational challenges, not predetermined technologies.

The initiative is at an early stage: we are developing our research methodology and building relationships with businesses.
